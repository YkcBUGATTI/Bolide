The MeteoriteLe Météore

From 19th-century French — bolide: a meteor that crosses the atmosphere at speed and burns out on its way to earth.

From Concept to Production — 2020 → 2024

From a blank sheet to a drivable car took less than a year. Designers and engineers simulated maximum loads, acceleration and lap times in a virtual environment — then built it.

At first it was "a drivable experimental vehicle." The enthusiasm of customers worldwide changed that. In August 2021, at The Quail in Monterey, Bugatti announced: the Bolide will be produced — 40 units.

The recognition followed quickly.

In 2021, an expert jury at the Festival Automobile International in Paris voted it the most beautiful hypercar in the world. In 2022, it took the Design Award at the Concorso d'Eleganza Villa d'Este.

The juries saw the same thing: every surface of this car is working. Not a single line exists for decoration — that is its beauty.

2020 · 10

The Online Reveal

A film · A sentence

No stage, no show — the Bolide appeared in a film, opening with "The Bugatti Bolide is real." Two days later it arrived in CSR Racing 2, letting the world press its throttle on a phone screen.

2021 · 08

The Production Decision

40 units · 4m euros

At The Quail in Monterey, Bugatti announced a limited production run. Customer demand made the decision — every reservation became a track machine built to its owner's will.

2024 · Onward

Deliveries Begin

Every car is finished by hand in Molsheim, from paint to interior, to the same standard as any collectible Bugatti. The only difference — this one was born for the track.

The W16, Unleashed8.0 L — Quad Turbo

Race track optimization means recalculating everything.

FIA LMH/LMDh — Le Mans Prototype Standards
0Horsepower
0Nm Torque
0Kg Dry Weight
0.91KG/HPWeight-to-Power

The W16 — the heart of every road-going Bugatti for twenty years — is pushed to its final form in the Bolide.

Turbochargers and lubrication systems were redesigned to withstand higher temperatures and forces; intake and exhaust were de-throttled for faster response and a new W16 symphony. The engine sits in a newly developed carbon monocoque, positioned 60 mm further forward than in the Chiron — packaging built for the track.

The monocoque was developed to the demanding FIA LMH/LMDh requirements — the same standards as Le Mans prototypes — in the world's highest-performing carbon material. Safety is not an add-on; it is part of the engineering.

The manufacturing is archival-grade, too.

A 3D-printed titanium pushrod weighs just 100 grams and transmits forces up to 3.5 tonnes — precision as fine as 0.1 mm. The X-shaped bracket in the cockpit, 3D-printed in aluminum, weighs 300 grams and withstands loads beyond 700 kg.

Before any Bolide meets its owner, it passes a final inspection and circuit testing program at Mirecourt — the first time Bugatti built its own quality process for a car with no benchmark.

Reduced to DrivingThe Cockpit

The cockpit draws its inspiration from LMP1 prototypes — everything unrelated to driving was asked to leave.

Monocoque — LMP1 Inspired

The wraparound cockpit keeps only the wheel, the pedals, one essential screen, and the driver.

This is restraint in service of driving — every material, every stitch exists so the driver can focus on the car alone. Even entering the cockpit was designed with care: the outer sections of the wraparound headrest fold away for easier access.

A Life Written on AsphaltTrack Chronicle

From the Le Mans centenary to its owners' first full throttle — this is the trail it left behind.

Circuits of the World — 2023 → 2026
2023 · 04 — Next Phase

The prototype enters the next phase of testing

Two and a half years earlier, the Bolide was an online film from autumn 2020. Now the prototype began high-intensity development testing on real circuits — after countless simulated laps, it was the asphalt's turn to speak.

2023 · 06 — Le Mans

Public dynamic debut at the centenary

At the 100th anniversary of the 24 Hours of Le Mans, the Bolide made its first public dynamic appearance. Behind the wheel: Andy Wallace — Le Mans winner, Bugatti Pilote Officiel.

2023 · 07 — Goodwood

Hillclimb debut at Goodwood

At the Festival of Speed, the Bolide roared up the famous hillclimb. For the first time, spectators heard the W16 fully unleashed.

2024 · 08 — Laguna Seca

Type 35 centenary edition × Laguna Seca

At Monterey Car Week, the centenary-edition Bolide shared the asphalt with the Type 35 that inspired it — a century after the Type 35's first race. One blue, one black, two timelines reunited on the hills of Laguna Seca.

2025 · 03 — Paul Ricard

Inaugural 'Feeling The Track'

The first owner track event took place at Circuit Paul Ricard. The same day, Bugatti unveiled Équipe Pur Sang — a dedicated Bolide liveried in Bugatti's racing colors.

This was the moment we had all been working toward — witnessing multiple Bolide examples unleashed on the track together.
FIRST DELIVERY — COTA
2025 · 01

The first owner, on the Circuit of the Americas, in the rain

An American owner drove his freshly delivered Bolide at COTA — a Christmas gift to himself. It rained that day. His expression when he returned to the pits said everything: "Incredibly easy to handle. The surge of power is incomparable."

MIAMI — 2026
2026 · 03

Miami: eight Bolides together

The largest gathering of Bolides to date. At first light at the Miami International Autodrome, eight meteorites lined up in the F1 pit lane — the W16's roar the only language spoken. Professional coaching and motorsport-level technical support, tailored to every owner.

A Vision Becomes Legacy40 Units — Molsheim Atelier

In November 2025, the final Bolide was completed at the Molsheim Atelier.

Molsheim — 2020 → 2026

Finished in iconic French Racing Blue with Nocturne accents, the last car was commissioned by its owner as a tribute to Bugatti's racing history.

Its completion closes the project: from the online reveal of 2020 to the final delivery, the W16's ultimate form ends with a run of 40.

With the end of the Chiron era, the Bolide and the W16 Mistral are the last Bugattis powered by the W16. After the Bolide, twenty years of this legendary engine enter history — in its purest, most uncompromising form.

For the owners, the journey is just beginning.

A worldwide network of Certified Bolide Centers — from pre-delivery inspection to annual servicing — is staffed by technicians trained to exacting standards.

And the annual owner track events, from Paul Ricard to Miami, are becoming a new tradition. Racing dreams are fulfilled here, then turned into stories.

40 cars, 40 completely different stories. Their only common point: the night a car rolled out of the garage in Molsheim.

We set out to create a car that can perform on the racetrack yet also belongs in the world's finest collections.
