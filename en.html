The MeteoriteLe Météore

From 19th-century French — bolide: a meteor that crosses the atmosphere at speed and burns out on its way to earth.

From a blank sheet to a drivable car took less than a year. Extreme loads, acceleration and lap times were validated in simulation first, then turned into metal.times virtually — then built it.

In December 2020, photos and film were released. It began as "a drivable experimental vehicle"; in August 2021, at The Quail in Monterey, Bugatti announced production: 40 units, at four million euros each.

The W16, Unleashed8.0 L — Quad Turbo

Race track optimization means recalculating everything.

0Horsepower
0Nm Torque
0Kg Dry Weight
0.91KG/HPWeight-to-Power

The W16 powered every road-going Bugatti for twenty years. In the Bolide, it is re-engineered for the track.

Turbochargers and lubrication systems were redesigned for higher temperatures and forces; intake and exhaust were de-throttled for faster response. The powertrain sits 60 mm further forward than in the Chiron, optimizing track weight distribution. The monocoque meets FIA LMH/LMDh requirements — the same standards as Le Mans prototypes.

Manufacturing: building its own standard for a car with no benchmark.

A 3D-printed titanium pushrod weighs 100 grams and transmits up to 3.5 tonnes, with 0.1 mm precision; the X bracket weighs 300 grams and carries more than 700 kg. Every Bolide passes final inspection and circuit testing at Mirecourt.

The Purity of Track DrivingThe Cockpit

The cockpit draws its inspiration from LMP1 prototypes — everything unrelated to driving was asked to leave.

The wraparound cockpit keeps only the wheel, the pedals, one essential screen, and the driver.

Restraint in service of driving — every material and stitch serves the drive. Entry was designed with equal care: the outer sections of the wraparound headrest fold away for easier access.

A Life Written on TrackTrack Chronicle

From the Le Mans centenary to its owners' first full throttle — the trail is all on asphalt.

2023 · 04 — Next Phase

The prototype enters the next phase of testing

In autumn 2020 the Bolide was an online film; two and a half years later the prototype was running high-intensity development on real circuits — simulation done, time for the real thing.esting on real circuits — virtual simulation complete, physical validation begins.

2023 · 06 — Le Mans

Public dynamic debut at the centenary

At the centenary of the 24 Hours of Le Mans, the Bolide made its public dynamic debut. At the wheel: Andy Wallace — Le Mans winner, Bugatti Pilote Officiel.

2023 · 07 — Goodwood

Hillclimb debut at Goodwood

At the Festival of Speed, the Bolide completed its hillclimb debut. For the first time, the W16 was heard in full.

2024 · 08 — Laguna Seca

Type 35 centenary edition × Laguna Seca

At Monterey Car Week, the centenary-edition Bolide shared the asphalt with the Type 35 — exactly a century after the Type 35's first race.

2025 · 03 — Paul Ricard

Inaugural 'Feeling The Track'

The first owner track event took place at Circuit Paul Ricard. The same day, Bugatti unveiled Équipe Pur Sang — a dedicated Bolide liveried in Bugatti's racing colors.

This was the moment we had all been working toward — witnessing multiple Bolide examples unleashed on the track together.

Sighting laps, convoy driving, individual preparation — multiple Bolides running together for the first time, with Bugatti technicians on hand throughout.

FIRST DELIVERY — COTA

The first owner, on the Circuit of the Americas, in the rain

An American owner drove his freshly delivered Bolide at COTA — a Christmas gift to himself. After the rain-soaked laps, his verdict: "Incredibly easy to handle. The surge of power is incomparable."

The largest gathering of Bolides to date. At first light at the Miami International Autodrome, eight Bolides lined up in the F1 pit lane. Professional coaching and motorsport-level technical support, tailored to every owner.
