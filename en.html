DOC.01 2020 — VIRTUAL REVEAL

The MeteoriteBOLIDE — LE MÉTÉORE

October 28, 2020. No stage, no motor show, no audience. In an online film, a machine never seen before rolls out of the Molsheim garage and roars into the night. Bugatti called it "an extreme, experimental design study."

The name comes from 19th-century French — bolide: a meteor that crosses the atmosphere at speed and burns out before falling to earth. It is the exact portrait of this car: built for the track, performance as the only purpose, no compromise, no mercy.

Eleven days later, a second film was released with a single line as its title: "The Bugatti Bolide is real." — Not a vision. A car that drives.

DOC.03 NÜRBURGRING · LE CASTELLET · IMOLA

AerodynamicsFORM FOLLOWS PERFORMANCE

"Every technical consideration has been translated directly into an aesthetic design." — Frank Heyl, Deputy Design Director at Bugatti. Not a single line of the Bolide exists for decoration: every surface generates downforce.

In testing, the Bolide produced up to 2.5 G lateral and nearly three tons of downforce depending on speed. Under hard braking the nose drops, bringing the front splitter closer to the ground and adding downforce — yet the engineers deliberately cap the front axle's downforce to preserve aero balance under braking. Work of this kind usually only exists in the top level of motorsport.

Specially designed air curtains ahead of the front wheels guide the airflow around the car and reduce drag; winglets on the splitter's outer edges create vortices that energize the flow toward the diffuser; side intercoolers sit in deep intakes to keep the engine at optimum temperature. Even the mirrors — Bugatti chose physical mirrors over cameras, because drivers judge distances faster.

The rear wing adjusts to each circuit's characteristics, letting every owner tune the balance between downforce and drag — motorsport-grade aero, available to forty people.

This extensive aerodynamic work usually only takes place in the top level of motorsport, but has allowed us to meticulously develop the Bolide for ultimate performance and drivability.
DOC.04 8.0 L — QUAD TURBO — 16 CYLINDERS

MachineryTHE W16, UNLEASHED

The W16 — the heart of every road-going Bugatti for twenty years — meets its purest form in the Bolide: race track optimized.

Turbochargers and lubrication systems were redesigned to withstand higher temperatures and forces; intake and exhaust were de-throttled for faster response and a new W16 symphony. The engine sits in a newly developed carbon monocoque, positioned 60 mm further forward than in the Chiron — packaging built for the track.

The monocoque was developed to the demanding FIA LMH/LMDh requirements — the same standards as Le Mans prototypes — using a super-high-strength carbon material, the highest performing of its type in the world. Before any Bolide meets its owner, it passes a final inspection and circuit testing program at Circuit de Mirecourt.

To pass these stringent but critical safety tests, we needed to ensure that everything about the monocoque was technically perfect so that on the track the Bolide is a race car that is safe and secure in every driving situation and environment.
DOC.05 MONOCOQUE — CENTRAL COCKPIT

Cockpit · The XTHE X THEME

X — the signature Bugatti took from the X-planes of aviation history: visible from every perspective.

X-shaped taillights frame the striking central exhaust; the cockpit melds around the same daring X-theme as the rear; the seats carry the X in multi-pad stitching. In the details, a 3D-printed aluminum X bracket weighs just 300 grams yet withstands loads beyond 700 kg.

The cockpit itself is inspired by LMP1 prototypes: reduced, minimal, made for driving. At rest, the steering wheel becomes a piece of sculpture.

DOC.07 40 UNITS — MOLSHEIM ATELIER

The FinalTHE FINAL BOLIDE

November 2025. The final Bolide was completed at the Molsheim Atelier.

It marks the end of a chapter: the W16's purest expression, concluded in 40 units. From an online reveal in 2020 to the last delivery, the car completed its full journey from "thought experiment" to collector's item.

Finished in iconic French Racing Blue with bold Nocturne accents — a customer's choice that pays homage to Bugatti's illustrious racing history.

The idea of being a perfect track car for both gentlemen and professional drivers is not so easy to translate into driving attributes, but it's essential to what makes it a Bugatti.
We set out to create a car that can perform on the racetrack yet also belongs in the world's finest collections.
